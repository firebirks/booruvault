The whole vault can be saved as one encrypted file from Settings > Vault, and restored on the first screen of a new install. A download cut short is taken up where it stopped when its host allows, downloads start in the order they were queued, and one asked for during Tor's exit check waits instead of failing. A full image that fails to load says why and offers a retry. Large vaults no longer rewrite their index every few downloads. Layout fixes in Browse, Downloads and the built-in browser.
