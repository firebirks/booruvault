The built-in and hidden browsers run on GeckoView, Firefox's engine, inside the app: WebRTC is off, with Tor on every request goes through it, pages run in isolated processes and sessions keep nothing. Through Tor they ask in English and tell the UTC time zone. One APK per architecture, about 95 MB. A page can no longer crash the app with a cookie, nor pass off a clearance it wrote, and a check is no longer taken as passed while still on.
