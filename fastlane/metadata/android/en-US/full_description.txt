BooruVault keeps the pictures you save from booru image boards in an encrypted vault and, if you want, reaches every site through Tor.

Everything the app stores is encrypted: the files you download, your settings, your sources and their API keys, site cookies and the download queue. Nothing opens without the vault password. At first start you choose Tor, through Orbot, or a direct connection; with Tor, all traffic goes through it, with no direct fallback.

Vault
• A password hardened with Argon2id, optional fingerprint unlock, auto-lock after a time in the background.
• Downloads are sealed to the vault's public key while they stream, so they can land even while the vault is locked and plaintext never touches storage.
• Collections by tag query, filters, import from the device, export in clear on request.
• Screen protection against screenshots and the recent-apps preview.

Tor and privacy
• Tor via Orbot, fail-closed: no direct fallback, no local DNS, and the exit is verified before any traffic leaves.
• Cookies, anti-bot passes and TLS sessions are kept apart for Tor and for the direct connection, so neither route shows what the other was handed.
• No image cache on disk; locking forgets the session; every keyboard is asked not to learn.
• Options that trade privacy for convenience are off by default, each one explaining its cost before it can be turned on.
• No Google Play services needed, no telemetry, no crash reports.

Browsing
• Safebooru, Safebooru.org, Konachan.net and e926 are ready to use; any board that speaks the Danbooru, Moebooru, Gelbooru 0.2 or e926 API can be added from its address, and Gelbooru 0.1, Shimmie2 and plain web pages are read from their pages.
• Tag autocomplete, favourite and most-used tags per source, an infinite grid, a content filter by rating and blacklist. Only general content is shown until you choose otherwise; questionable and explicit ratings ask for an adult's confirmation.
• A viewer with zoom, video playback and a tag sheet.
• A built-in browser to pass anti-bot checks, log in or set site preferences; it stays on the site it was opened for, and the cookies it brings back are kept in the vault.

Downloads
• Single posts, a selection or a whole search, in the background. The queue survives a lock and a restart.
